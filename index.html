What I audit

A thorough passive scan.
No credentials, no intrusion.

Every check uses ordinary HTTP requests — the same view a visitor has. No login, no authenticated access, no probing beyond what your site sends publicly.

01
HTTPS & transport
Confirms encryption is active and properly enforced. An unencrypted site leaks visitor data and hurts search rankings.
02
Security headers
Six browser-level protections most websites skip. Each missing header is a gap that could be exploited.
03
CSP quality
Having a Content Security Policy isn't enough — a misconfigured one can cancel out its own protection. I check for the common mistakes.
04
Form exposure
Contact forms and input fields deserve extra scrutiny. I check whether they're exposing information they shouldn't be.
Going further
Full active scan available
The passive audit is always free and requires no access. For businesses that want to go deeper — with written permission I can run a full active scan: input validation testing, injection probing, and authenticated flow analysis. This surfaces vulnerabilities a passive scan can't see.

The grading system

One letter.
Instant read.

Every audit produces an indicative grade based on the checks performed. It reflects what a passive scan can see — not the complete security posture of a website.

A
No issues found
Nothing flagged by the passive checks. Good baseline — not a guarantee of complete security.
B
Minor gaps
A few low-severity issues. No immediate risk, but worth addressing.
C
Worth fixing
Medium-severity issues present. Common — several headers missing.
D
Needs attention
High-severity issues. Visitors may be exposed to real risks.
E/F
Act now
Multiple serious gaps flagged. Worth treating as urgent and reviewing with a developer.
